# K1 — Evidence-to-Money Infrastructure

## What K1 is

K1 is the trusted **evidence-to-money infrastructure layer** for construction and critical infrastructure.

We turn field events into **financial-grade truth** — records strong enough to authorize payment, release retainage, lift liens, price risk, and back tokenized payment rights.

## How it works

A job happens in the field. K1 captures it, validates it, and converts it into something the rest of the world can trust and act on.

1. **Field event** — work happens, or it doesn't.
2. **Validated evidence** — captured on ruggedized devices, spatial context, cryptographic sealing.
3. **AI + human scoring** — multi-modal fusion plus mandatory human validation.
4. **Immutable record** — tamper-evident, agency-aware, long-term archivable.
5. **Workflow, compliance, risk** — change orders, approvals, regulatory gates.
6. **Financial impact** — cash flow, forecasting, risk-premium pricing.
7. **Payment / settlement action** — authorization, escrow, lien waiver, retainage release, tokenized rights.

Same record. Whole chain.

## Why this matters

Construction and critical infrastructure currently rely on **unverifiable field reporting to authorize huge sums of money**. Change orders are disputed, draws are slow, retainage sits, liens get filed, risk is mispriced. K1 closes that gap by making the evidence itself financial-grade.
