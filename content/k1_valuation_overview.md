# K1 Valuation Overview

**Founder:** William E. Branley
**Positioning:** Trusted evidence-to-money infrastructure layer for construction and critical infrastructure.
**As of:** 2026-05-25

## One-line thesis

K1 turns field evidence into financial-grade truth. The same record that proves a job was done correctly is the record that authorizes payment, releases retainage, lifts liens, prices risk, and — increasingly — backs tokenized payment rights.

## Headline IP estate

- **16 clean utility nonprovisional filings**, spanning platform, field OS, controlled evidence, critical infrastructure, change orders, four regulated verticals (nuclear, semiconductor, defense, corrections), and a six-filing financial stack.
- **17 provisionals** strategically covering the full Infinity Curve category.
- **2 mismatched duplicate filings** (19/686,754, 19/686,755) flagged for counsel cleanup — not centered in narrative.

## Value drivers (why these ranges are credible)

1. **Layered IP estate, not a feature list.** Lead platform, core field OS, controlled evidence, critical infrastructure, change-order core, four regulated verticals, and a six-filing financial stack.
2. **Regulated-vertical coverage** in segments where switching costs are real: nuclear power, semiconductor fabs, defense / classified, corrections.
3. **A real financial surface.** 6 of 16 clean nonprovisionals — over a third of the estate — are direct financial-stack patents: settlement, lending, receivables, fraud, credit, escrow, lien waivers, retainage, milestone billing, forecasting, and tokenized rights.
4. **Defensible category position** as the "evidence-to-money infrastructure layer" — not another point tool.
5. **Codified founder knowledge.** Concentration risk mitigated by a structured IP estate that survives independent of the founder.

## What is K1 worth — to whom?

- **ConstructionTech platforms.** Defensible moat + regulated-vertical coverage they cannot replicate quickly. Multi-year roadmap leapfrog.
- **Vertical SaaS acquirers.** Acquire category ownership of evidence-to-money infrastructure.
- **Lenders / surety / payment networks / B2B fintechs.** Evidence-validated lending draws, receivables verification, fraud detection, contractor credit, lien-waiver and retainage automation, and tokenized payment rights reduce loss content and unlock new credit and liquidity products.
- **Insurance / risk carriers.** Dynamic risk-premium pricing and validated chain-of-custody reduce claims leakage.
- **Defense / critical infrastructure primes.** Compliance-grade evidence and chain-of-custody for multi-decade capex programs.
- **Public-market strategics in adjacent stacks.** A natural bolt-on layer that turns existing workflows into settlement-grade flows.

## How the ranges move

- Add a paid pilot in a regulated vertical → **post-MVP** range.
- Add multi-customer recurring revenue → **early revenue** range.
- Add measurable dollars authorized, lent against, or settled via K1 → **finance/payment traction** range.
- Add a strategic buyer with a clear synergy thesis (loss-content reduction, category control, vertical leverage) → **strategic acquisition** range, open-ended at the top.

## Bottom line

K1 is priced today as an **IP + founder + roadmap** story at $10M–$15M pre-money. Each subsequent stage is mechanically supported by the same evidence-to-money thesis encoded in the patent estate. The financial stack — six filings deep — is what converts construction-tech valuation logic into fintech valuation logic at the upper ranges.
