How K1 Works

1. Capture

Superintendents and field teams capture photos, videos, notes, and sensor data directly on site — even offline.

2. Validate

Every record requires mandatory human validation on site. No unverified data enters the system.

3. Seal

Records are cryptographically sealed in real time, creating an immutable chain of custody.

4. Compound

Through the Infinity Curve, every validated capture makes the entire project record stronger over time.

The Infinity Curve

Evidence that gets stronger, not weaker, over the life of a project.

Most documentation degrades the longer a project runs. K1 flips that. Every human-validated capture compounds the strength of your entire evidence record — making change orders and payment claims more defensible the longer they sit.
