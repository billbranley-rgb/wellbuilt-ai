Validated distance cannot slide backward

The infinity loop has a thread — and a lock.

Read the curve vertically. Field truth enters at the bottom. Every validated event is a turn of the thread that lifts proof one click upward. A locking collar — the K1 ratchet — closes behind it. The record can advance. It cannot retreat.

  • One-way travel. Vaulted records do not silently change, disappear, or revert.
  • Locked turns. Each validated event is the unit of compounding — a click on the ratchet.
  • Distance is the asset. Years from now, the height on the rod is the proof your project owns.

The problem

Skilled labor was hired to build.
Not to become note-takers.

Construction burns too much time turning field reality into paperwork. The problem is not that contractors lack forms — it is that skilled workers get pulled into secretarial work while decisions, time, and money are already moving.

Autodesk/FMI’s Construction Disconnected report found construction professionals spend 35% of their time — over 14 hours per week — on non-productive activities like searching for information, conflict resolution, mistakes, and rework.

Why current tools fall short

After the fact

Most tools manage the change-order process after the entry is already made, after the cost is already in motion.

  • Field event is captured in the trailer, not at the work
  • Skilled workers become note-takers instead of staying on skilled work
  • Photos, voice notes, and paperwork are uploaded hours or days later
  • Time, location, and reviewer trail are reconstructed
  • Cost, schedule, and contract references are added by memory
  • Change-order package is assembled after the leverage is gone
Real-time validated

K1 gives the field an active voice while the work is still controllable.

  • Capture at the work — photo, video, voice, location, time
  • Responsible person and reviewer trail attached at the source
  • Human Validation Gate before anything is treated as record
  • Cost, schedule, spec, and contract references linked in
  • Owner-grade control package before the die is cast

Project lifecycle

Field event to risk model.
Validated events flow into project controls and commercial workflows, seal at closeout, and feed forward into future estimating and risk models.

  1. M01
    Access Gate

    Pre-Site Verification

    K1 will not activate until required jobsite credentials and agency approvals are verified. Credentialed site access is the first gate.

  2. M02
    Activation Gate

    Live Bind

    User, device, project, role, and workflow are bound together at activation. If any of those drift, K1 forces a re-verify.

  3. M03
    Field Capture

    Voice, photo, metadata

    Captured on rugged devices and parsed for safety and commercial relevance — the raw field signal before anything becomes a record.

  4. M04
    Human Validation Gate

    Permitted human authorizes

    Captured events promote to commercial records only when a permitted human validates them. No human, no record.

  5. M05
    Evidence Vault

    Immutable chain of custody

    Raw capture is preserved and the validated record is sealed on top — time-stamped, signed, chain-of-custody intact.

  6. M06
    Workflow Routing

    Downstream integration

    Validated events route into RFIs, Change Orders, Safety Logs, Project Controls, and Cost Engines — schedule and budget update from the sealed record.

  7. M07
    Control Panel

    PM / Exec operating view

    A validated-only operating view. Every KPI on the panel is derived from a sealed record — no unvalidated noise reaches the executive layer.

  8. M08
    System Architecture

    Field intelligence

    Patent-pending four-gate architecture and chain of custody — the structural backbone that lets closeout feed forward into future estimating and risk models.

Closeout that compounds

Commercial workflows, project controls, and closeout all draw from the same sealed records. When the job ends, the evidence doesn't retire — it feeds the next estimate and the next risk model.
